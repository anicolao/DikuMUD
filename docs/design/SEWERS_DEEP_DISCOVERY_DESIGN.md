### Develop Core Storyline

**The History of the Lesser Helium Sewers:**

Over a thousand years ago, when Lesser Helium was first constructed, the city's founders built an elaborate waste reclamation and water management system. The Martian engineers of that era were masters of their craft, creating not just functional sewers but an underground marvel with hidden maintenance passages, valve systems for flood control, emergency drainage mechanisms, and secure storage compartments for tools and equipment.

The system featured sophisticated water control mechanisms: large valve wheels that could redirect flow, drain flooded sections, or seal off damaged areas. Maintenance crews accessed these systems through locked compartments built into the tunnel walls, secured with iron keys to prevent tampering.

When Lesser Helium expanded centuries later, a newer, more advanced sewer system was built to serve the growing population. The ancient sewers were officially decommissioned, their access points sealed, their maintenance tunnels abandoned. Over time, the sealed compartments were forgotten, the valve systems seized from disuse, and the engineering diagrams were lost or stored away in dusty archives.

**The Current Situation:**

Now, the ancient sewers serve as a home for creatures that have adapted to the dark, damp environment:

- **Ulsio vermin** infest the upper tunnels, breeding rapidly in the abundant filth
- **Carrion birds** have discovered openings to the surface and nest in the higher chambers, bringing scraps of meat from their scavenging runs above ground
- **Giant spiders** have woven elaborate webs in side passages, their silk remarkably strong
- **Slime molds and fungi** thrive on the organic matter accumulating in the passages
- **Banths**, drawn by the scent of prey, occasionally venture into the deeper sections

The janitors of Lesser Helium are well aware of these sewers. They enter regularly to maintain what they can, clearing blockages and keeping the worst of the filth from backing up into the city above. Over the years, they've heard legends from older workers about "the old mechanisms" and "sealed rooms with ancient tools," but most dismiss these as tall tales.

However, one particularly curious janitor (mob 3061) has been exploring during his shifts. He's noticed things: locked panels in tunnel walls bearing maintenance symbols, seized valve wheels that clearly once served a purpose, strange markings suggesting hidden passages. He's even found fragments of old technical diagrams among debris in side tunnels. He suspects there's more to these sewers than anyone realizes, but he lacks the skills or equipment to investigate properly.

**The Deep Discovery Storyline:**

A thorough explorer who takes the time to examine the environment carefully can uncover the secrets of the ancient sewer system:

1. **Discovery of the Maintenance System**: Examination of the water channels reveals maintenance access panels built into the walls, marked with ancient Martian engineering symbols

2. **The Rope Connection**: Spider webs in the eastern passages contain exceptionally strong silk that can be harvested as rope - the same type the ancient engineers used for their mechanical systems

3. **The Sealed Compartments**: Locked maintenance compartments require iron keys, which have been scattered over the centuries. Carrion birds, attracted to shiny objects, have collected some of these keys in their nests

4. **The Valve Mechanism**: In a deeper chamber, an ancient valve wheel mechanism still stands, designed to operate with rope-and-leverage system. When activated, it drains a flooded lower passage

5. **The Hidden Cache**: Behind the drained section lies a sealed engineering cache containing ancient tools, technical diagrams, and access to even deeper, unexplored sewer levels

6. **The Reward**: Players who solve this puzzle gain access to unique ancient engineering equipment, lore about Lesser Helium's history, and entry to previously inaccessible areas with rare encounters

### Define Deep Discovery Objectives

**For Casual Players (Just Passing Through):**

Players who simply want to complete basic objectives:
- Enter sewers from Lesser Helium waste disposal (room 3030 to 3150)
- Navigate main passages
- Fight ulsio vermin (Quest 3001)
- Kill carrion birds to collect dried meat (Quest 3003)
- Collect basic loot (pelts, bone fragments, rusty weapons)
- Exit back to city
- **Time Investment**: 15-20 minutes
- **Rewards**: Quest completion (500 XP, 125 gold total), basic loot

**For Deep Discovery Players (Thorough Explorers):**

Players who examine everything and seek hidden content:

1. **Examine the Water Channels**: Discover maintenance panels in walls
2. **Examine Spider Webs**: Learn that rope can be harvested from the strong silk
3. **Search Carrion Bird Nests**: Find iron key(s) among debris
4. **Examine Maintenance Panels**: Discover they're locked, learn what key is needed
5. **Unlock Compartments**: Use iron key to access sealed storage
6. **Find Ancient Tools**: Discover maintenance equipment and technical diagrams
7. **Examine Drainage Systems**: Notice valve wheel mechanism in deeper chamber
8. **Read Technical Diagrams**: Learn how rope-and-lever system operates the valve
9. **Use Rope with Valve**: Activate ancient mechanism to drain flooded passage
10. **Access Hidden Areas**: Enter previously inaccessible lower levels
11. **Discover Ancient Cache**: Find unique equipment and lore items
12. **Optional Quest**: Return discoveries to curious janitor for additional rewards

**Time Investment**: 45-60 minutes
**Rewards**: 
- Unique ancient engineering tools (valuable or useful equipment)
- Access to new zone areas with rare spawns
- Lore items explaining Lesser Helium's history
- Optional quest rewards (if janitor quest implemented)
- Achievement/satisfaction of puzzle-solving

### Storyline Summary

The ancient sewers beneath Lesser Helium were built over a thousand years ago as a sophisticated water management system with hidden maintenance passages, valve controls, and secured tool compartments. When the city expanded, newer sewers were built and the ancient system was abandoned. Now creatures have moved in: ulsio vermin, carrion birds bringing scraps from above, giant spiders, and banths.

A curious janitor in Lesser Helium has noticed evidence of the old engineering systems but lacks the means to investigate. Players who thoroughly explore can discover maintenance compartments, harvest rope from spider webs, find iron keys in bird nests, and activate ancient valve mechanisms to drain flooded passages and access hidden caches of tools and lore.

### Room Special Procedure System Design (YAML-based)

**Requirement**: Create a data-driven system for room-based item interactions without hardcoding specific items into C code.

**Design Concept**:

Instead of creating specialized C functions for each puzzle (like `spec_ancient_rope` or `spec_maintenance_valve`), we define a generic room special procedure system that reads configuration from YAML.

**YAML Schema Addition**:

```yaml
rooms:
  - vnum: 3157
    name: "Deep Sewer Chamber"
    # ... existing fields ...
    
    # NEW FIELD: special_interactions
    special_interactions:
      - trigger: "use"  # Command that triggers this
        items: [3224, 3220]  # Objects that work (rope or spider silk)
        keywords: ["valve", "wheel", "mechanism", "rope"]
        success_message: |
          You loop the rope through the pulley above the valve wheel and pull
          with all your might! The rope provides the leverage needed, and with
          a screech of protesting metal, the ancient valve begins to turn.
          Water rushes through pipes all around you as the mechanism activates.
        failure_message: "You need something to provide leverage for the valve."
        room_message: "$n uses a rope to activate an ancient valve mechanism!"
        consume_item: true  # Rope is used up
        change_room_state: "valve_activated"  # Sets a flag on the room
        effects:
          - type: "modify_room"
            target_room: 3160
            change: "sector_type"
            from: 6  # SECT_WATER_NOSWIM
            to: 0    # SECT_INSIDE
          - type: "message_to_room"
            target_room: 3160
            message: "You hear a rumbling sound as water begins to drain away!"
```

**Alternative Simpler Design** (for initial implementation):

Since a full data-driven system requires significant C code changes, we specify a compromise approach:

1. **Create generic "use item with room feature" command** that checks for:
   - Player has specific item(s) in inventory
   - Player is in specific room
   - Optional: Keyword matching

2. **Define interactions in YAML** (for documentation and future automation):
```yaml
# In sewers.yaml metadata section
puzzle_interactions:
  - room: 3157
    command: "use rope valve"
    requires_item: [3224, 3220]
    consumes_item: true
    result: "Valve activated, room 3160 drained"
    
  - room: 3151  
    command: "use key panel"
    requires_item: 3232
    consumes_item: false
    result: "Compartment opened, spawns objects 3233 and 3234"
```

3. **Implement C special procedures** that read room vnum and item vnums from these definitions:
   - `spec_room_item_interaction()` - generic handler
   - Looks up room/item combinations in loaded YAML data
   - Executes appropriate effects

**For This Design Phase**: We document the intended behavior and defer implementation details to coding phase. The key design principle is: **interactions should be data-driven, not hardcoded**.

### Difficulty and Balance Analysis

**Clue Difficulty**: 
- **Subtle but Fair**: Clues are present in environment descriptions
- **Progressive**: Start with broad hints, get more specific
- **Redundant**: Multiple paths to success (rope OR silk, multiple bird nests)
- **Documented**: Diagram provides explicit instructions once found

**Puzzle Difficulty**:
- **Moderate**: Requires thoroughness but not obscure knowledge
- **Solvable**: All information needed is in-game
- **Logical**: Steps follow naturally from clues
- **No Dead Ends**: Each clue leads to actionable next step

**Combat Difficulty**:
- **Unchanged**: Same enemies as before
- **Level Appropriate**: Zone is level 1-3 content
- **Optional Fights**: Can navigate around some encounters

**Reward Balance**:
- **Proportional**: 3x time investment, ~2x rewards
- **Fair**: 1125 gold + items for 45-60 minutes is reasonable
- **Unique**: Items not available elsewhere (tool set, diagram)
- **Not Overpowered**: Gold is useful but not game-breaking
- **Memorable**: Puzzle solution more valuable than loot

**Time Balance**:
- **Casual**: 15-20 min = Quick quest completion
- **Deep Discovery**: 45-60 min = Extended exploration session
- **Reasonable**: Both time investments appropriate for content depth
